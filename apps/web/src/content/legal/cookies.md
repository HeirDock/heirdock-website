## Overview

This Cookie Notice explains the cookies and similar technologies HeirDock LLC ("HeirDock," "we," "us," or "our") uses on our website at [heirdock.com](https://heirdock.com) (the "Website"), in the HeirDock web app at [app.heirdock.com](https://app.heirdock.com), and in the HeirDock app for Android. It forms part of our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy), and uses the terms defined there.

The short version: we use only what HeirDock needs to work. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, so there is nothing to opt in to or out of.

---

## 1. What Cookies and Browser Storage Are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser. Local storage and session storage are similar: they let a web page keep small pieces of information in your browser. Session storage is cleared when you close the tab; local storage stays until it is removed.

---

## 2. On the Website

Browsing the Website sets no cookies.

When you send a form on the Website (such as Contact Us or Request a Demo), the page loads Google reCAPTCHA to tell people from automated spam. reCAPTCHA sets one necessary cookie (`_GRECAPTCHA`) and sends information about your device and how you used the page to Google, which processes it on our behalf.

## 3. In the Web App

### 3.1 Cookies

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Lifetime |
|--------|--------|---------|----------|
| Session cookie | HeirDock | Keeps your connection to HeirDock secure while you sign in and use the app | 2 hours, renewed as you use the app |
| `XSRF-TOKEN` | HeirDock | Protects against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) | 2 hours, renewed as you use the app |
| `__stripe_mid`, `__stripe_sid` | Stripe | Fraud prevention when you add or change a payment method | Up to 1 year (`__stripe_mid`) and 30 minutes (`__stripe_sid`) |

### 3.2 Browser Storage

The web app also keeps some information in your browser's storage:

- **Your sign-in:** keeps you signed in. It is removed when you sign out.
- **Your preferences:** such as your theme, text size, and how you like lists displayed.
- **Conveniences:** such as your recent searches, the last place you were working, and drafts of forms you have not saved yet, so you do not lose your work. Signing out removes these.
- **Single-session information:** such as an invitation you are accepting, a sign-in with Google in progress, unsaved Quick Add progress, and copies of what you have viewed, so pages load faster. This is kept in session storage and cleared when you sign out or close the tab.

### 3.3 Google Fonts

The web app loads its icons from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address and browser details when it does, but Google Fonts does not set cookies. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. In the HeirDock App for Android

The app does not use cookies. It keeps your sign-in in your phone's secure storage (the Android Keystore), and keeps photos waiting to upload in its private storage until they are sent. See Sections 1.2 and 1.4 of our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for what the app collects.

---

## 5. What We Do Not Use

We do not use analytics cookies, advertising or retargeting pixels, session recording or heatmap tools, or social media tracking cookies on the Website, in the web app, or in the HeirDock app for Android.

If we ever add a cookie that is not essential, we will update this notice first and, where the law requires, ask for your consent before setting it.

---

## 6. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you see, block, and delete cookies and stored data in their settings. Because everything we use is essential, blocking it may stop you from signing in to the web app or sending a Website form.

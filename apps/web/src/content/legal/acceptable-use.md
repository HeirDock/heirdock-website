## 1. Intended Use

The Platform is designed to help households and institutions organize, document, value, and share structured information about household assets. You may use the Platform only for its intended purposes, including: cataloging and documenting household items and personal property; uploading photographs, descriptions, and attachments related to your items; storing legal documents (wills, trusts, deeds, insurance policies) in the encrypted Vault; managing valuations, Intentions, and Beneficiaries; sharing Household Records with authorized Trusted Participants and Professionals; and using AI Features to assist with categorization, descriptions, and estimated valuations.

## 2. Your Content

### 2.1 What You May Store

You may store content that relates to the documentation of household assets and related estate, insurance, and financial planning purposes. This includes photographs of personal property, item descriptions and metadata, financial records such as valuations, purchase receipts, and insurance details, legal documents such as wills, trusts, deeds, and Powers of Attorney, family and household information necessary for managing your Household Record, and life event supporting documentation as required by the Platform.

### 2.2 Accuracy

You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide. You agree not to knowingly upload false, misleading, or fraudulent information to the Platform, including fabricated valuations, forged documents, or misrepresented item descriptions.

### 2.3 Rights to Content

You must have the legal right to upload and share any content you place on the Platform. You agree not to upload content that infringes the intellectual property rights, privacy rights, or other legal rights of any third party.

## 3. Prohibited Activities

You agree not to use the Platform to:

### 3.1 Illegal or Harmful Activity

- Engage in any activity that violates applicable local, state, national, or international law.
- Store, transmit, or distribute content that is illegal in your jurisdiction.
- Facilitate or promote fraud, money laundering, tax evasion, or any other financial crime.
- Use the Platform in connection with any scheme to defraud insurers, lenders, or other parties, including inflating asset valuations for fraudulent insurance claims.

### 3.2 Misuse of Life Event and Designation Features

- Falsely report a life event (such as the passing or incapacitation of a Primary Account Holder) in order to gain unauthorized access to a Household Record.
- Submit forged, fabricated, or materially misleading documentation to support a life event transition.
- Manipulate Steward or Care Steward designations to circumvent the rightful wishes of a Primary Account Holder.
- Use the life event transition process to interfere with or obstruct legitimate estate proceedings.

### 3.3 Unauthorized Access and Security Violations

- Access or attempt to access another user's account, Household Record, or data without authorization.
- Share your account credentials with others or allow others to use your account. To give someone access, invite them as a Trusted Participant instead.
- Attempt to circumvent, disable, or interfere with the Platform's access controls, role-based permissions, tenant isolation, or other security measures.
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Platform or any related system or network.
- Introduce viruses, malware, worms, Trojan horses, or other harmful code to the Platform.

### 3.4 Abuse of Platform Resources

- Use automated tools (bots, scrapers, crawlers, or similar) to access the Platform or extract data without our written permission.
- Use the Platform in a manner that degrades performance or availability for other users.
- Interfere with, disrupt, or place an undue burden on the Platform's infrastructure.
- Circumvent or attempt to circumvent any rate limits, usage quotas, or storage limits.
- Use the API in a manner that violates the API documentation or rate limits.

### 3.5 Prohibited Content

You may not upload, store, or transmit content that:

- Contains child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or exploits minors in any way.
- Promotes violence, terrorism, or the commission of violent crimes.
- Contains malware, viruses, or other harmful software.
- Is obscene, defamatory, or constitutes unlawful harassment or threats.
- Violates export control or sanctions laws.

The Platform is not designed for and must not be used to store: classified government information, protected health information (PHI) subject to HIPAA, payment card data subject to PCI DSS (note: payment processing is handled exclusively by Stripe), or any data whose storage would require compliance certifications that HeirDock does not hold.

### 3.6 Commercial Misuse

- Resell, sublicense, or otherwise commercially exploit access to the Platform without our prior written consent.
- Use the Platform to build a competing product or service.
- Use HeirDock's name, logo, or marks in a manner that suggests endorsement or affiliation without our written permission.
- Use the Platform to send unsolicited communications or advertisements.

### 3.7 Misrepresentation

- Impersonate another person, including another user or a HeirDock employee.
- Misrepresent your identity, authority, or affiliation when accessing a Household Record or submitting a life event transition.
- Create multiple accounts to circumvent account restrictions or to evade enforcement actions.

If you access the Platform as a Professional (institutional user such as an estate planner, attorney, financial advisor, or insurance professional), you additionally agree to:

- Access client Household Records only within the scope of consent granted by the Account Holder.
- Not copy, download, or extract client data from the Platform for purposes beyond the scope of your authorized access, except as required by law.
- Maintain the confidentiality of all client data you access through the Platform.
- Comply with all applicable professional conduct rules, ethical obligations, and data protection laws governing your use of client data.
- Not use client data obtained through the Platform for marketing, solicitation, or any purpose unrelated to the services you provide to that client, unless separately authorized by the client.

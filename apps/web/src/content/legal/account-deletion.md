You can delete your account for the HeirDock app and HeirDock on the web at any time. HeirDock is made by HeirDock LLC. You don't need to sign in to read this page.

## How to delete your account

**In the HeirDock app:** open **Me**, then **Security**, then **Delete account**.

**On the web:** sign in at [app.heirdock.com](https://app.heirdock.com), open **My Account**, then **Security**, then **Delete account**.

**By email:** write to [privacy@heirdock.com](mailto:privacy@heirdock.com) from the email address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account.

---

## What happens

- You are signed out on every device straight away.
- You have 30 days to change your mind. Sign in during that time and choose **Keep my account**.
- After 30 days, your account and your personal information are deleted. This can't be undone.
- If you are the Account Holder of a Household Record that no one else uses, it is deleted with your account. If other people use it, HeirDock support will contact you first to help decide what happens to it.
- If a household you look after is in Care Mode or Stewardship, please contact HeirDock support and we will help with the next steps.
- Copies in our backups are deleted within 30 days after your account is deleted.

---

## What stays

- What you added to someone else's Household Record, as a Manager or with Shared Access, such as notes and photos, stays in that household's Record.
- Billing records, kept as tax and accounting law requires, and audit records (which hold account identifiers and IP addresses, but no names or content). Both are linked to an anonymized record, not to you.
- Security logs, which hold account identifiers, IP addresses, and the web addresses requested (including search words), but no other content: up to 90 days for application logs, 30 days for load balancer logs.
- Crash reports from the app, held by our crash reporting provider (Sentry) for its standard retention period.
- Emails you have sent to HeirDock support, kept as long as we need them to help you.

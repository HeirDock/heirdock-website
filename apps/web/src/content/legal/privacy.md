## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you provide to us, information collected automatically, and information from third-party sources.

If you only visit the Website, we collect much less: what you submit through a form, and the log and form-protection data described in Section 1.2.

### 1.1 Information You Provide

**Account Information.** When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and authentication credentials (password hash, TOTP secrets). If you add a phone number or a profile photo, we collect those too; once text messages are available, we may use your phone number to send you security codes. We never share your phone number, or your agreement to receive text messages, with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

**Household Record Content.** When you use the Platform, you may provide:

- Item descriptions, names, categories, tags, and notes
- Photographs of household items and property
- Audio recordings you make to tell an Item's story
- Documents, receipts, and links you add to Items and their records
- Purchase details, such as the price you paid and the date you bought or received an Item
- Valuation information, including estimated values, purchase prices, and insurance details
- Notes, questions, answers, and stories you or others in your household add
- Legal documents uploaded to the Vault, such as wills, trusts, deeds, insurance policies, and estate planning documents
- Intentions for items (Keep, Sell, Gift, Donate, or Undecided) and who each is Intended For
- Information about people in your household, including names, relationships, and contact information
- Collection structures, Beneficiaries, and sharing preferences

**Designations and Life Events.** If you name a Steward, Alternate Steward, Care Steward, or Beneficiary, we collect who they are (as one of the people in your Household Record) and whether they have accepted. If a life event is reported in the Platform, we collect the type of event and any notes. HeirDock Support may ask for documents, such as a death certificate or a Power of Attorney, when handling a life event; what you send them is kept with your support request.

**Payment Information.** When you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not directly collect or store your full card number. Stripe provides us with limited card information: the card brand, the last four digits, and the expiration date. For more information, see [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

**Website Forms.** When you write to us through a Website form, we collect your name, your email address, and your message, plus: on Contact Us, the topic you choose; on Request a Demo, your company, your role, and roughly how many clients you serve; on Request Access, your practice's name and type. You are not required to send a form, but we cannot reply without this information.

**Communications.** If you contact us for support or provide feedback, we collect the content of your communications, your email address, and any attachments.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access and use the Platform, we automatically collect the information below. When you visit the Website, we collect only the Website access logs and form-protection data described under Log Data and Form Protection.

**Usage Data.** The actions you take in the Platform (such as adding or changing an item), and sign-in records: the time, your IP address, and the browser or app you used.

**Device and Technical Data.** Your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, device identifiers, language preferences, and time zone.

**Phone App Data.** If you use the HeirDock app for Android, we also collect an install identifier the app creates for each installation (so you can see and sign out your devices), your phone's model and Android version, the app's version, and a push token if you allow notifications. The app does not use advertising identifiers or third-party analytics.

**Log Data.** Our servers log each request to the Platform. Our application logs record the web address without its query (the part after "?"), the method, the response status, and the time, and refer to people only by user and household identifiers. Our load balancer also keeps an access log of each request: the full web address, which can include what you typed in a search box, your IP address, and the user agent of your browser or app. Load balancer logs are kept for thirty (30) days. For the Website, our hosting provider (AWS) keeps standard access logs of each page request: your IP address, browser type, the page requested, the referring web address, and the time. Website access logs are kept for up to one (1) year.

**Form Protection.** Website forms use Google reCAPTCHA to tell people from automated spam. When you send a form, reCAPTCHA collects information about your device and how you used the page (such as your IP address and browser details) and sends it to Google, which processes it on our behalf to protect our forms from spam, fraud, and abuse.

**Cookies and Similar Technologies.** We use only essential cookies and browser storage, such as those that keep you signed in to the Platform. See Section 8 and our [Cookie Notice](/legal/cookies) for details.

### 1.3 Information from Third-Party Sources

**Google OAuth.** If you register or sign in using your Google account, we receive your name and email address from Google, as permitted by your Google account settings.

**Stripe.** Stripe may provide us with transaction status, payment confirmation, and limited billing details as described above.

**Future Integrations.** If you connect third-party services (such as insurance providers, financial services, or wealth advisory platforms) through the Platform's integration features, we may receive data from those services as authorized by you. We will update this Privacy Policy to describe specific integrations as they become available.

### 1.4 The HeirDock App for Android

This section explains what the app uses on your phone and what it sends to us.

- **Camera and photos.** The app uses the camera only when you take a photo of an Item, a document, a label, or yourself, and your photo library only when you pick photos. Before a photo leaves your phone, the app saves a new copy without its location and other camera details (EXIF data).
- **Microphone.** The app uses the microphone only while you record a story about an Item. The recording is uploaded to your Household Record.
- **Documents.** When you add a document, the app sends only the file you pick.
- **Search.** What you type in a search box is sent to our servers to find results. It can appear in our load balancer logs, which are kept for thirty (30) days (see Section 1.2).
- **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, the app registers a push token with us. We send notifications through Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Notifications on your lock screen never show names, Items, or values. You can turn notifications off by type in the app, or for HeirDock in your phone's settings.
- **App lock and biometrics.** If you turn on the app lock, your phone checks your fingerprint, face, or PIN. Your biometric data stays on your phone. HeirDock never receives it.
- **Storage on your phone.** Photos waiting to upload, and Items you are adding, are kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which Android encrypts. They are deleted once they are uploaded, or when you sign out. The app's data is not included in Google cloud backups and is not moved to a new phone.
- **Crash reports.** When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry, our crash reporting provider. A report contains your user identifier, your phone's model and Android version, the app's version, the screens you opened before the error (by screen type, without names or identifiers), and technical details about the error. Before a report is sent, the app removes names, email addresses, phone numbers, search words, Item details, sign-in tokens, and amounts. Sentry is set not to store IP addresses.

## 4. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

### 4.1 Data Storage Location

All Platform data is stored in Amazon Web Services (AWS) data centers located in the United States; the Website and Platform are delivered to you through AWS's network, which has locations worldwide. If you access the Services from outside the United States, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

### 4.2 Security Measures

We implement technical and organizational security measures designed to protect your data, including:

- **Tenant isolation:** PostgreSQL row-level security ensures that all queries are filtered by household, preventing cross-tenant data access.
- **Encryption at rest:** Vault documents (wills, trusts, deeds, insurance policies, and other sensitive documents) are encrypted using AES-256, with a separate encryption key for each document, protected by AWS Key Management Service (KMS). Other data at rest is encrypted using AWS-managed encryption.
- **Encryption in transit:** All data transmitted between your device and the Platform, and between the Platform and its sub-processors, is encrypted using TLS.
- **Media access controls:** Photos and other files are served through signed links that expire within fifteen (15) minutes. Vault documents are sent to you directly by our servers, after you confirm your identity.
- **Audit logging:** State changes, mode transitions, consent changes, and security-relevant operations are logged in structured, append-only audit logs. Audit logs hold user and household identifiers and IP addresses, but no names, email addresses, or Household Record content.
- **Integrity verification:** Mode change events are linked via a SHA-256 cryptographic checksum chain. Household data snapshots taken at each mode transition are stored with their own SHA-256 hash for integrity verification.
- **Access enforcement:** Multi-layered access controls including middleware role checks, compound scope resolution, and database-level row-level security.
- **Authentication security:** Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app (TOTP), available with every sign-in method and required for Professionals. Uploaded files are scanned for malware.

### 4.3 Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

- **Active account data:** Retained for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Platform's services.
- **Archived items:** When you remove an item from your Household Record, it is archived rather than erased, so it can be restored. Archived items are kept with the Household Record.
- **Archived Household Records:** A Household Record archived after its subscription lapsed is kept, closed, until the Account Holder deletes their account; HeirDock Support can restore it.
- **Deleted accounts:** You can delete your account in the HeirDock app (Me, then Security, then Delete account), on the web, or by emailing [privacy@heirdock.com](mailto:privacy@heirdock.com). You are signed out on every device straight away, and you have thirty (30) days to change your mind by signing in and choosing Keep my account. After thirty (30) days, your account and your personal information are deleted. If you are the Account Holder of a Household Record that no one else uses, that Household Record is deleted too; if other people use it, HeirDock support will contact you first to help decide what happens to it. What you added to someone else's Household Record, such as notes and photos, stays with that household. Backup copies are retained for up to thirty (30) days for disaster recovery purposes, after which they are permanently destroyed. Our [account deletion page](/legal/account-deletion) explains each step.
- **On your phone:** Photos waiting to upload and Items you are adding are deleted from your phone once they are uploaded, or when you sign out of the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept on your phone.
- **Audit logs and mode change events:** Retained for the life of the Household Record (including after transition to Archived mode) to maintain data integrity and the checksum chain.
- **Life event documents:** Documents sent to HeirDock Support about a life event are kept with the support request, for as long as we need them to handle it and keep a record of it.
- **Payment and audit records:** Transaction records are retained as required by applicable tax, accounting, and financial reporting laws. When an account or Household Record is deleted, its billing records and its audit records (which hold identifiers and IP addresses, but no content) are kept, linked to an anonymized record rather than to you.
- **Sign-in records:** Kept until your account is deleted.
- **Feature usage:** A record of each use of Asset Analysis, Suggestions, or Market Valuation (which feature, when, which model, and the outcome, with none of your content) is kept for thirteen (13) months.
- **Crash reports:** Kept by Sentry for its standard retention period, then deleted.
- **Server logs:** Application logs are kept for up to ninety (90) days, load balancer access logs for thirty (30) days, and Website access logs and our AWS account's activity logs for up to one (1) year, for security monitoring and troubleshooting, then deleted.
- **Website form messages:** Messages you send through a Website form are delivered to our team by email and kept for up to one (1) year after your request is resolved, unless a longer period is required by law. The name and email address from each form are also recorded in our server logs, which are kept for up to ninety (90) days.

### 4.4 Data Export

The Account Holder and Managers may export Household Record data at any time before the Household Record is archived, through the Platform's built-in data export feature. We recommend exporting your data before deleting your account.

### 4.5 Data Portability

Upon request, we will provide your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format to the extent required by applicable law.

## 5. AI Data Processing

### 5.1 What Data Is Processed by AI

When you use AI Features, the following data may be sent to our AI infrastructure for processing:

- Item descriptions and text details, such as names, categories, condition, and notes
- Photographs of items, including photos of serial-number labels
- For Market Valuation, the price you paid and when you bought the item
- For review questions about a Vault document, only the type of document (never its contents)

This data is processed to identify and categorize items, suggest descriptions and details, read serial numbers, check how complete a record is, and estimate market values. In the Platform, these features are called Asset Analysis, Suggestions, and Market Valuation; we refer to them here as AI Features.

### 5.2 How AI Processing Works

Our AI Features use generative AI models from Amazon and Anthropic, run inside Amazon Web Services through Amazon Bedrock, in the United States.

**Key data protections for AI processing:**

- **No model training:** Amazon Bedrock does not use your inputs or outputs to train any foundation models. Your data is never used to improve or fine-tune AI models.
- **No data sharing with model makers:** Your data stays within AWS and is not sent to Anthropic or any other model maker.
- **No retention:** Amazon Bedrock does not store your requests or its responses, and we have not turned on Bedrock's optional request logging. We keep the results you accept as part of your Household Record.
- **Encryption:** All data sent to and from Amazon Bedrock is encrypted in transit using TLS.

### 5.3 AI-Generated Outputs

AI-generated valuations, descriptions, and categorizations are estimates and informational aids. They are not professional appraisals, certified valuations, or legal advice. See our [AI Disclosure](/legal/ai-disclosure) for more information.

### 5.4 When AI Features Run

Analysis runs when you use Quick Add, Asset Analysis, Suggestions, or Market Valuation, and refreshes after you edit an item that has already been analyzed. If you do not use these features, your data is not sent for analysis. There is not yet a setting to turn them off for your account; we may add one.

## 6. Sharing and Access Controls

### 6.1 How Sharing Works

The Platform allows you (as the Account Holder) to share your Household Record with Trusted Participants and Professionals. When you share data, the recipients can see the portions of your Household Record within their assigned scope and role. You control all sharing through the Platform.

### 6.2 Trusted Participant Access

Trusted Participants are either Managers, who see and manage the whole Household Record on your behalf, or people with Shared Access, who see only the Collections and Vault documents you share with them, each at the access level you choose (Viewer, Contributor, or Full Access). You may change or revoke their access at any time.

### 6.3 Professional (Institutional) Access

Professionals see only what you consent to share with them. If you allow it, an insurance Professional can also add and update your insurance policies, coverage, and claims; every change is recorded, and you or a Manager can undo it. Professionals keep their own notes, flags, and comments about your account in their workspace. You or a Manager can change or revoke a Professional's access at any time, and every consent and change is logged in the Platform's audit trail.

### 6.4 Life Event Access Changes

During life event transitions, access may change as described in our Terms of Service. In summary: during Stewardship, the designated Steward becomes a Manager; during Care Mode, the Care Steward acts as a Manager and the Account Holder's access becomes view-only until Care Mode ends. Other participants keep their existing access.

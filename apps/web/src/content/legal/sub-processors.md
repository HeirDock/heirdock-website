## Overview

HeirDock LLC ("HeirDock") uses a limited number of third-party service providers ("Sub-Processors") to help operate the HeirDock website, the HeirDock application, and the HeirDock app for Android. Each Sub-Processor is contractually required to protect your data in accordance with our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) and applicable data protection laws.

This page lists all Sub-Processors that may process personal data or user content on behalf of HeirDock.

## AI-Specific Data Protections (Amazon Bedrock)

Amazon Bedrock is designed with the following data protections, which apply to all AI processing:

- Your inputs and outputs are **not used to train** any foundation models.
- Your data is **not shared** with third-party model providers (including Anthropic).
- Your data is **not stored** by Bedrock, and we have not turned on its optional request logging.
- All data is **encrypted in transit** using TLS.

For details, see the [Amazon Bedrock data protection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-protection.html) and our [AI Disclosure](/legal/ai-disclosure).
